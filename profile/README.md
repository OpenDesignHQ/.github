## Build with OpenDesign

Bring a brief, a reference, or an idea. Work alongside your team and AI agents to turn it into something you can use, share, and refine.

Explore our open-source projects below. Their source code, issues, and releases are maintained in the linked repositories under [nexu-io](https://github.com/nexu-io).

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### [OpenDesign](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design)

**Your collaborative AI design workspace.**

Create prototypes, websites, apps, decks, images, and video with your team and AI agents. Share feedback and iterate in the same project. Extend your workflow with skills, design systems, templates, and plugins.

[Explore the workspace →](https://open-design.ai/)

### [openmotion](https://github.com/nexu-io/openmotion)

**The open-source Claude Motion &amp; CapCut alternative.**

Turn a reference video into editable motion graphics with your own brand kit. Recreate scenes, generate media, refine timing in Studio, and export an MP4. Bring your own agent and keys.

[Explore openmotion →](https://open-design.ai/motion/)

<a href="https://github.com/nexu-io/openmotion"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nexu-io/openmotion?style=flat&amp;label=Stars&amp;color=24292f" alt="openmotion GitHub stars" /></a>

### [html-video](https://github.com/nexu-io/html-video)

**Programmatic video for coding agents.**

Turn HTML, CSS, and data into real MP4s on your laptop. Start with a template, choose a render engine, and add an AI soundtrack. Open source, with no per-render fees.

[Explore html-video →](https://open-design.ai/html-video)

<a href="https://github.com/nexu-io/html-video"><img src="https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nexu-io/html-video?style=flat&amp;label=Stars&amp;color=24292f" alt="html-video GitHub stars" /></a>

### [motion-anything](https://github.com/nexu-io/motion-anything)

**Describe the feeling. Your AI ships the animation.**

Create animations and edit motion on running HTML pages, component by component. Explore motion recipes and export CSS, React, Lottie, MP4, GIF, and more.

[Explore the motion engine →](https://github.com/nexu-io/motion-anything#readme)

## Join the community

- **Build with us.** Contribute code, skills, design systems, templates, and documentation to the project you use.
- **Share feedback.** Report a bug or suggest an improvement in the relevant repository's Issues.
- **Meet other builders.** Join [Discord](https://discord.gg/mHAjSMV6gz), follow [@OpenDesignHQ](https://x.com/OpenDesignHQ), or reach us at [support@open-design.ai](mailto:support@open-design.ai).
